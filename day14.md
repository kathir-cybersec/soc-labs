 Day 14 – Scenario-Based SOC Analysis

 What I Learned

* Scenario-based questions are important for SOC interviews
* Learned how to think like a SOC analyst in real situations
* Understood the 4-step approach: Identify, Investigate, Contain, Report

---

 Scenarios I Practiced

* Brute force attack (multiple failed logins)
* Phishing email analysis
* Unusual outbound network traffic
* Malware alert from SIEM
* Suspicious admin login activity

---

 Approach I Used

* Identify: Understand what the issue could be
* Investigate: Check logs, IPs, and activity
* Contain: Block or isolate the threat
* Report: Document and escalate

---

 Hands-On Practice

* Practiced answering real SOC interview scenarios
* Simulated incident response thinking
* Spoke answers out loud for better understanding

 My Thoughts
