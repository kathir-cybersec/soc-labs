 Day 12 – SIEM Basics

 What I Learned
- SIEM collects and analyzes logs from different systems
- It helps detect security threats and generate alerts
- SOC analysts use SIEM to monitor and investigate incidents

 How SIEM Works
- Collects logs from systems and networks
- Analyzes data for suspicious activity
- Generates alerts for analysts

 Example Scenario
- Multiple failed login attempts detected
- Possible brute force attack
- Action: Investigate logs and source IP

 Tools
- Splunk (basic understanding)

 My Thoughts
- SIEM is very important for SOC jobs
- It makes monitoring easier and faster
