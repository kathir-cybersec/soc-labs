 Day 11 – SOC Scenarios and Analysis

 Scenario 1 – Brute Force Attack
- Multiple failed login attempts detected
- Possible brute force attack
- Action: Investigate IP and block if malicious

 Scenario 2 – Suspicious Process
- Unknown script running in system
- Could be malware
- Action: Analyze process and check source

 Scenario 3 – Suspicious Network Traffic
- Connection to unknown IP on unusual port
- Could indicate malicious activity
- Action: Check IP reputation and monitor traffic

 What I Learned
- SOC analysts investigate alerts step by step
- Not all activity is malicious, investigation is important

 My Thoughts
- I am learning how to think like a SOC analyst
